# Changelog

All notable changes to the AURIORA organization profile are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Released versions are tagged in version control.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-04

### Changed

- Standards paragraph now mentions that the standards scale requirements with project maturity.
- Areas of Exploration now states the initial experimental focus (plants and the fungal and mycorrhizal networks they form) while keeping the broader comparative scope.
- Framing widened from "living systems" and "forms of life" to "very different systems" in the opening description, the Vision question and the Why section, matching the website 1.2.0; the Vision question is now the website's "What is intelligence, and how does it emerge in different systems?". "Living" is kept where the text describes the actual biological focus and the instruments.
- "Parametric audio" module family renamed to "Acoustic stimulation" and described as programmable sound stimuli from synthesized patterns and stored audio samples.
- "Plants and living networks" area rephrased as a question (how organisms without a centralized nervous system sense, coordinate and adapt) instead of asserting intelligence in plants, matching the website.
- "What We Are Building" notes that module repositories are published as each module reaches a releasable state.
